## Direction contract
THESIS: An official-game-site layout done at studio craft. Imagery leads every viewport; type is big, white and quiet; motion is cinematic. It refuses neon glow, gradient text and novelty fonts.
OWN-WORLD: True black ground, white type, a soft-white secondary. Archivo variable sets everything: heavy and wide for display, regular for body. Solid white primary buttons with black text; thin white outline secondaries; square corners on media with a 2px radius at most.
