# Product

## What it is
An unofficial, fan-made showcase site for Grand Theft Auto VI, built by Rizwan Waseem as a practice
project in cinematic front-end motion. Deployed on Vercel. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

## Audience and job
GTA fans and portfolio reviewers. Fans come to rewatch the official trailers, explore the
characters and the state of Leonida, and find the release date and editions. Reviewers judge the
craft: scroll choreography, transitions, type, and performance.

## Truth rules
- Facts (release date, platforms, editions, prices, characters, places, trailer list) come only from
  Rockstar's own channels or reporting that cites them. Anything fan-written is labelled as such.
- Official trailers are embedded from Rockstar's YouTube channel only, never re-hosted.
- Background music is royalty-free and never Rockstar or trailer audio.
- Every page carries the fan-project disclaimer.

## Brand commitments
- The home intro (the city revealed through the letters VI, which then zooms past the camera) is the
  soul of the site and stays.
- Pricedown is the display voice. The palette is the Vice City sunset of the existing logo and art.
- The site names itself as unofficial and does not present itself as Rockstar.

## Platforms
Web, desktop through phone (320px to 2560px). Touch and reduced-motion visitors get full content.
